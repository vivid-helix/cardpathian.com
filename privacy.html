Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Cardpathian is developed by Vivid Helix Inc. ("we"). This policy covers the playtest telemetry in pre-release builds of Cardpathian. The short version: playtest builds send anonymous gameplay statistics that cannot identify you or be linked to you, and there is an off switch in the pause menu.

What playtest builds send

Playtest builds send anonymous gameplay events:

  • Progress milestones — research and operations completed, regions and biomes found, cards and traits discovered, recipes performed for the first time, quest recipes completed.
  • Session start, session end, and session length.
  • A periodic summary including the rough number of cards you had in play at once.
  • Game version, operating system family, and GPU vendor.
  • A random session identifier, generated fresh each time the game starts and linked to nothing — not to you, your hardware, or your other play sessions.

What we never collect

No name, no email, no location, no account, no chat, no personal information of any kind. Nothing identifying is ever stored on your machine. Your IP address is used only to deliver the data and is discarded at ingestion — it is not stored, and no location data is derived from it. Because sessions carry no shared identifier, events cannot be linked to you or connected across play sessions. We never sell or share the data with anyone.

Why we collect it

One purpose: improving the game during playtesting — seeing where players get stuck, which recipes get used, and how long sessions last.

Where it goes

Data is processed by PostHog and stored on servers in the European Union, in anonymous mode: no person profiles are ever created. We keep it only as long as it is useful for development.

Your choices

Playtest builds send data by default — it is anonymous, so there is nothing to ask you about. If you would rather send nothing, turn off the "Send anonymous playtest data" toggle in the in-game pause menu; sending stops immediately and the choice is remembered.

Why there is no "delete my data" process

We hold nothing that identifies you — no account, no identifier, no profile — so there is no way for us to find "your" records, and data-protection rights that attach to personal data do not arise here. If you have questions or concerns anyway, email us and we will do our best to help.

Children

Cardpathian is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
