Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Cardpathian is developed by Vivid Helix Inc. ("we"). This policy covers the optional playtest telemetry in Cardpathian. The short version: the game sends nothing unless you explicitly say yes, and nothing it ever sends can identify you.

What we collect (only with your consent)

When you accept the in-game question, the game sends anonymous gameplay events:

  • Progress milestones — research completed, regions and biomes found, cards and traits discovered, recipes performed for the first time, quest recipes completed.
  • Session start, session end, and session length.
  • A periodic summary including the rough number of cards you had in play at once.
  • Game version, operating system family, and GPU vendor.
  • A random install identifier and a per-session identifier. These are random numbers generated on your machine; they are not derived from you or your hardware.

What we never collect

No name, no email, no location, no account, no chat, no personal information of any kind. Your IP address is used only to deliver the data and is discarded at ingestion. We never sell or share the data with anyone.

Why we collect it

One purpose: improving the game during playtesting — seeing where players get stuck, which recipes get used, and how long sessions last. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.

Where it goes and how long it stays

Data is processed by PostHog and stored on servers in the European Union. It is deleted after 13 months.

Your choices

The game asks once, before anything is collected. Decline and nothing is ever sent. You can change your answer at any time with the "Send playtest data" toggle in the in-game pause menu; turning it off stops collection immediately.

Your rights

You can request access to, or deletion of, the data associated with your install. The pause menu shows your install id — quote it in an email to privacy@vividhelix.com and we will handle the request. Without that id we cannot identify your records, because we hold nothing else about you. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

Cardpathian is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears at this address with an updated date above.
